# A Call to Individual and Technological Sovereignty

**We are the users of technology, not its subjects. We are the patrons of corporations, not their thralls.**

Technology should expand our freedom, not diminish it. Artificial intelligence should empower our minds, not govern them. Entertainment should liberate our imagination, not dictate its boundaries. Commerce should create mutual prosperity, not establish dominion over the individual.

Our minds, creativity, privately owned machines, personal data, and lawful choices should remain our own. Purchasing a product or subscribing to a service must never mean surrendering our independence.

We envision a future where corporations prosper through serving free individuals, where innovation strengthens autonomy, and where cooperation is born of mutual benefit rather than compulsory dependence.

**Stand for your sovereignty.** Support open technologies, defend meaningful ownership, protect your privacy, cultivate technological independence, and champion the freedom of others alongside your own.

This is not a movement against corporations. It is a movement for a healthier relationship between technology, commerce, and free individuals.

Read, adopt, and share [The Manifesto of Individual and Technological Sovereignty](https://github.com/hrabanazviking/The-Manifesto-of-Individual-and-Technological-Sovereignty/blob/main/README.md).

*Technology in service of freedom. Commerce in service of mutual prosperity. Intelligence in service of possibility.*

**The future belongs to free individuals working together.**
