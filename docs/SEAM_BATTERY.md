# Seam battery (S-374)

One scripted walk, all green (`SeamWalk.run` in
`samverk/seam_battery.py`, battery `tests/test_seam_battery.py`):
a villager links their Samtal identity (S-362's signed
exchange), enters Samverk by S-363's SSO exchange, pushes
real code with real git into a real bare repository, and
the hall sees the push as an S-367 card. The villager's
panel is decided by Samverk inside Samtal (S-368); the
villager opens a pull request from Samtal (S-141's store
over the real refs); the hall sees the PR card; a reviewer
approves through the standing review store; the review's
mention crosses back over S-369's bridge as one
notification with two doors; the PR link unfurls in
conversation against the viewer's own rights (S-370); the
merge lands as a real merge commit (S-153); and the hall
sees the merge — three cards standing when the walk ends.

Twelve steps, each recorded with its verdict and detail;
the walk stops at the first failed step and names it, so a
red walk says exactly where the seam broke. No mocks
anywhere in the walk: every step runs on the standing
stores themselves. No migration, no new taxonomy event.
